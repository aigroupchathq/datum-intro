Who is behind Datum

Built with passion, by car people, for car people.

For everyone who sees a car as more than a machine: the early mornings, the long drives, the hands that kept it going, and the stories it carries from one keeper to the next.

Who

Datum Atelier is an independent project run by Vaishnav Deshmukh. It is a trading name, not a limited company.
