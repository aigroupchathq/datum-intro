07 · Engineering philosophy

Engineered to remember.
Designed to question.

A tamper-evident record helps detect modification. It does not certify ownership, workshop quality, odometer truth or condition. Datum keeps these stages apart, on purpose.

STAGE 1 · CURRENT FOCUS

Recorded

Structured entries with source, date and authorship. The foundation everything else stands on.

STAGE 2 · CURRENT FOCUS

Supported by evidence

Photographs, documents and measurements attached at source, separable from the claims they support.

STAGE 3 · PLANNED

Independently corroborated

Confirmation from separate qualified parties. A direction, not a capability, nothing here is certified today.

09 · Who we are

Built from a belief that automobiles
are more than machines.

Datum is an independent, founder-led automotive technology initiative, in development. It began with a passion for automobiles and an interest in connecting the engineering, history, care and human relationships around them. There is no corporate apparatus to announce, only work to show, and an invitation to shape it.

10 · Invitation

An invitation is earned the old way.

Datum is not bought into. It is recognised into. That recognition runs three ways. This is the whole of it:

01: YOU AND THE CAR

Custodianship

You give attention, honesty and care. The car gives you its years. What matters here is simple: only lived detail becomes history. Mileage with meaning, maintenance with receipts, love with evidence.

02: THE CAR AND DATUM

Record

The car gives facts. Datum gives permanence. What matters is evidence over claims and source over rumour, so the story can never be doubted, even decades on.

03: DATUM AND YOU

Trust

Datum gives privacy, control and understanding. You give nothing you did not choose to share. What matters is that the story stays yours. We keep it. We never sell it.

If you want in: don't tell us net worth or following. Tell us about a morning, a machine, and what your hands know. Collections counted, not driven, do not impress us.
