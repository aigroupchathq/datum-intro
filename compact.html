01, What Datum is

Without Datum, history rots. With Datum, it holds.

A car can be cared for over decades and still arrive with a story full of gaps.

“The receipt survived. The reason for the repair did not.”An illustrative problem, not a customer quote

Datum is a custodianship record for cars in development. The aim is to keep identity, service, evidence and memory attached to the vehicle, in one dated history that survives a handover.

It is not a marketplace, a social feed or a government registry. It is the layer underneath: a private record of what happened, who did the work, and what supports the claim.

01, RecordOne dated historyIdentity, ownership, mileage and service, kept in order.
02, EvidenceEvidence beside the entryInvoices, photographs and measurements kept beside what they support.
03, ContinuityPrivate by designSharing should always be the custodian's decision.

03, How it works

From garage to workshop, one record.

The proposed record connects the car, its custodian and its operating history. Three ideas and one journey.

01, Provenance↗ A history with its sourcesOwnership, service entries and supporting material belong on one dated timeline, with each claim kept distinct from its evidence.
02, Workshop↗ Workshop integrationA proposed QR passport would link the car to its profile. Work could be recorded at the ramp by the technician or owner, against the vehicle.
03, Ecosystem↗ More than a service bookWorkshop entries, private memories and the next handover belong in the same car's story, with permission deciding what travels.

The evidence ladder

Recorded, supported, corroborated.

Each stage is kept apart on purpose. The record does not claim more than it can support.

01 Current focus

Recorded

Each entry carries a source, a date and the person who made it.

02 Current focus

Supported by evidence

Photographs, invoices and documents sit beside the claim they support, separable from it.

03 Planned

Independently corroborated

A second qualified party confirms the work, so the same voice is never the only witness.

A record that resists tampering helps detect modification. It does not certify ownership, workshop quality, odometer truth or condition.
