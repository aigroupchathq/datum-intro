What this site collects today

Nothing you type. The invitation form is a preview: it does not send your name, email or message anywhere, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

What your browser keeps

  • Your choice of light or dark theme, saved in your browser's local storage as datum_theme so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.
  • The odometer on the dial counts your time on the page in memory only. It is not saved and resets when you leave.

Hosting and security

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the site, they process technical information about each request, such as your IP address and browser type. Cloudflare may run a security check and set a cookie that is strictly necessary to tell people from automated traffic.

Their own notices explain how they handle this: GitHub privacy statement and Cloudflare privacy policy.

When the invitation form goes live

Before the form collects anything, this notice will be updated to say exactly what is collected, why, how long it is kept and who handles it. Emails about Datum would only be sent if you tick a separate box to ask for them, and you could stop them at any time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Write to [Contact email]. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
