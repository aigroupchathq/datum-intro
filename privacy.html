Privacy and site information

Who runs this website, what it stores and sends, and what you can ask of us. Written for the site as it works today.

Who runs this site

Datum Atelier is an independent automotive software project in development. It is a trading name, not a limited company.

Operated by

Email

What this site collects today

Form status: preview. The invitation form does not send anything yet, and nothing you enter leaves your device. When it is switched on, this line will say so and give the date.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site, and your browser stores nothing for it.

If you request an invitation

Once the form is switched on, this is exactly what happens to what you send.

What we keep
Your email address, the option that describes you (keeper, collector, workshop or something else), the note you write if any, and whether you asked for news by email, with the exact wording you agreed to and the date.
Why
To invite you to try Datum and talk to you about it. If, and only if, you tick the separate news box, to send occasional news about Datum.
Lawful basis
Your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to www.vaishnavdeshmukh@gmail.com, and every news email will include a way to stop.
Where it is kept
In a database run by Cloudflare, which acts on our instructions as our processor. Cloudflare may process data outside the UK under the safeguards in its data processing terms.
Spam check
Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person is using the form. It loads only on the invitation page and processes technical information about your browser for that purpose. See the Turnstile privacy notice.
How long
Deleted automatically 24 months after you last sent the form, or sooner if you ask.
Who sees it
Only the person who runs Datum. It is not sold, published or shared.

Sending the form does not trigger an automatic email.

Hosting and security

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the site, they process technical information about each request, such as your IP address and browser type. Cloudflare may run a security check and set a cookie that is strictly necessary to tell people from automated traffic. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Write to www.vaishnavdeshmukh@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
