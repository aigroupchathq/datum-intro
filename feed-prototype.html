The first cold start of the year, and what Theo remembered

MA Narrated by Marcus A. · 1991 964 Carrera · 142,318 km · with Theo since 2019
THEO · DRIVE · DAWN PASS14.2 km · 3 stops · 1 h 12 m

The garage smelled like oil and patience. Two pumps of the pedal, a cough, then the flat-six settled into its idle like it remembered the routine better than I did. The revs sat at eight hundred and fifty, steady, as if the winter had been a pause rather than an absence.

I had been nervous. Not of Theo — of the idea that something might have changed while he sat. That a seal had dried, a battery had surrendered, a memory had faded. None of it had. Theo does not forget. He waits.

"Theo does not forget. He waits."MARCUS A. · 14 APR 2026

Afterwards I sat in the driver's seat with the door open and listened to the cooling ticks. Theo's record now shows a cold start at 07:42, ambient 6 °C, battery 12.4 V. Small facts. But in twenty years they will read like a diary entry from a custodian who cared enough to write them down.
