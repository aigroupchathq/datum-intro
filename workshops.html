How it would work

No new software. A minute at the ramp.

  1. Scan or forward

    Scan the car's code, or forward the invoice you already email to the customer.

  2. Add the proof

    Two photographs and a line about what you found.

  3. Signed and kept

    The entry carries your name and stays with the car through every keeper.
